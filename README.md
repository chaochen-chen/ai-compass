## About

This repo holds notes and discussions from our ongoing AI reading club. Currently a small experimental group, with room to grow into a broader community over time.

Topics covered include:
- AI supply chain
- Investment guidelines & trends
- Career development (e.g., salary, company's culture, interview tips, etc.)
- Other AI-adjacent topics as they come up
